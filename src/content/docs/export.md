## Send the document itself

A `.mdoc` document is a single file with everything inside: text, images and attachments. Attach it to an email or put it in a shared folder. Nothing gets left behind.

The other person needs MarkPlus to open it. For everyone else, use a PDF.

## Share a whole workspace

Want to send the whole project, every document with its pictures and files, to someone who doesn't have MarkPlus? Export the workspace as a web page:

1. Open the workspace you want to share.
2. Choose **Workspace › Export as Web Page…** (or **File › Export as Web Page…** while editing).
3. Pick where to save it. You get one file, for example `Coffee Handbook.html`.

Send that file by email, AirDrop or a shared folder. The other person double-clicks it and it opens in their web browser, on any computer, tablet or phone. Nothing to install:

- it opens on the workspace's home page, just like in MarkPlus: the cover, the icon, the [description](../workspaces/#add-a-description) and every document as a card. Click the workspace name on the left to come back to it;
- every document is listed on the left with its [icon](../documents/#document-icon): [pinned documents](../workspaces/#pin-documents) first, then documents and databases apart, with the pages of the one that's open;
- read a document all at once or [one page at a time](../appearance/#pages-continuous-or-one-at-a-time), with the buttons at the top right (**Continuous** or **Single page**). One page at a time, turn pages with <kbd>←</kbd> and <kbd>→</kbd> or the arrows at the bottom right. The browser remembers your choice;
- pictures, videos and attached files are all there;
- links between documents work, so readers can jump from section to section;
- [databases](../databases/), mentions (@) and data views work too;
- <kbd>⌘F</kbd> searches the document;
- the **Full Screen** button at the top right hides the browser's tabs and toolbars, for reading or presenting;
- nothing can be changed by accident: it's read only.

**Example:** you wrote a coffee shop handbook in five documents. Export the workspace and send `Coffee Handbook.html` to the new barista. They get the whole handbook in one file, with every link working.

:::tip
Give your workspace an icon and a cover (see [Workspaces](../workspaces/#add-an-icon)). The page shows them in the sidebar, and the browser tab shows the icon. On your Mac, the exported file also shows the icon in Finder.
:::

:::note
Pictures and attachments go inside the page, so a workspace with many photos or videos makes a big file. Some email services refuse attachments over 20–25 MB: for those, use a shared folder or a link from your cloud storage.
:::

## PDF

Choose **File › Export as PDF…** (<kbd>⌥⌘E</kbd>) and pick:

- **Paper**: A4 or Letter
- **Orientation**: portrait (standing) or landscape (lying down)
- **Page numbers** at the bottom of each page
- **Bookmarks**: a table of contents in the PDF's sidebar, made from your titles

Then choose where to save it. MarkPlus remembers your choices for next time.

### What you get

- Links to other parts of the same document **still work**: click and the PDF jumps to that page.
- Links to **other documents** turn into a note, for example "(See: Recipes › Espresso)".
- Web links open in the browser.
- Videos become a link to the video, and attached files show their name.
- Titles, images and tables aren't split across two pages.
- The PDF is always light, even if you write in dark mode.

:::tip
Want the lines on screen to match the PDF? Choose the **Narrow (A4)** width while you write. See [Width](../appearance/#width).
:::

## Markdown

**File › Export Markdown…** (<kbd>⇧⌘E</kbd>) saves a plain-text copy that almost any app can open. Simple formatting is kept. Attached files are not included.

## Copy into another app

- To paste into **Mail, Pages or Word** with the formatting, select the text, right-click and choose **Copy as Rich Text**.
- To paste into an app that uses Markdown, choose **Copy as Markdown**.
- To copy the **whole document**, choose **File › Copy as Markdown** (<kbd>⌥⌘C</kbd>).
