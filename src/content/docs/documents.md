## Create a document

Press <kbd>⌘N</kbd>, or click the **+** card in the document list. MarkPlus asks which format you want.

## .mdoc or .md?

| | **.mdoc** (recommended) | **.md** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Everything you write in MarkPlus | Notes you also open in other apps |
| Keeps | Everything: colors, image sizes, columns, videos, attachments, link previews | Text, titles, lists, tables, links and simple images |
| Images | Inside the document | In a folder next to it |
| Opens in other apps | No, only in MarkPlus | Yes, in any text editor |

**In short:** choose **.mdoc**, unless you need to open the file in another app.

In a `.md` document, the options it can't keep are turned off. Point at one and it says **Only available in .mdoc documents**.

:::tip
To switch a `.md` document to `.mdoc`, choose **File › Convert to .mdoc**. The old file goes to the Trash only after the new one is saved.
:::

A `.mdoc` is **one file** with everything inside: text, images and attachments. Copy it, move it or email it, and nothing gets left behind.

:::note
Documents from older versions of MarkPlus open normally. The first time you edit one, it's updated to the new format.
:::

## Find and open documents

The main screen lists every document in the workspace, most recently edited first. Dates read the way people talk: *Just now*, *Yesterday*, *Monday*. Click a document to open it.

- **Grid or list**: use the two buttons next to the workspace name. In the list, click **Name**, **Date Modified** or **Kind** to sort. Click again to reverse.
- **Documents from anywhere**: **File › Open…** (<kbd>⌘O</kbd>) opens a document from any folder, without moving it. You can also double-click it in Finder.
- **Open Recent**: the **File** menu remembers what you opened last.

## Tabs

Each open document gets a tab, just like in a web browser.

- **Switch**: click a tab, or press <kbd>⇧⌘]</kbd> and <kbd>⇧⌘[</kbd>.
- **Close**: click the **×** or press <kbd>⌘W</kbd>. Closing the last tab takes you back to the list.
- **Reorder**: drag a tab left or right.
- **Rename**: double-click the name in the tab, type, then press <kbd>↩</kbd>.
- **More options**: right-click a tab.

Each tab remembers where you were and what you can undo. Your tabs are still there the next time you open MarkPlus.

## Two documents side by side

Drag a tab out of the window and drop it anywhere: it opens in its own window. You can also right-click a tab and choose **Move to New Window**.

**Example:** keep the research notes on the left and the report you're writing on the right.

A document can only be open in one window at a time. If you try to open it again, MarkPlus takes you to the window where it already is.

## Rename and delete

- The **file name is the title**. Rename it in MarkPlus or in Finder, whichever you prefer.
- Right-click a document in the list for **Rename…**, **Open in New Window** and **Delete**.
- **Delete** moves the document to the Trash, so you can still get it back.

## Saving

You don't need to save. MarkPlus saves a couple of seconds after you stop typing, and again when you switch documents or quit. If you like, <kbd>⌘S</kbd> saves right away.

## The bar at the bottom

- **Left**: where the document is on your Mac. Click the last folder to open it in Finder.
- **Right**: how many characters, words and paragraphs it has, and whether an AI app is connected.

Turn each side on or off in **View › Path Bar** and **View › Status Bar**.
