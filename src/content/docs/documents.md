## Create a document

Press <kbd>⌘N</kbd>, or click **New document** above the document list. MarkPlus asks which format you want.

## .mdoc or .md?

| | **.mdoc** (recommended) | **.md** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Best for | Everything you write in MarkPlus | Notes you also open in other apps |
| Keeps | Everything: colors, image sizes, columns, text boxes, videos, attachments, link previews | Text, titles, lists, tables, links and simple images |
| Images | Inside the document | In a folder next to it |
| Opens in other apps | No, only in MarkPlus | Yes, in any text editor |

**In short:** choose **.mdoc**, unless you need to open the file in another app.

In a `.md` document, the options it can't keep are turned off. Point at one and it says **Only available in .mdoc documents**.

:::tip
To switch a `.md` document to `.mdoc`, choose **File › Convert to .mdoc**. The new file is created in the same folder as the old one, which goes to the Trash only after the new one is saved.
:::

A `.mdoc` is **one file** with everything inside: text, images and attachments. Copy it, move it or email it, and nothing gets left behind.

:::note
Documents from older versions of MarkPlus open normally. The first time you edit one, it's updated to the new format.
:::

## Find and open documents

The main screen lists every document in the workspace, most recently edited first. Dates read the way people talk: *Just now*, *Yesterday*, *Monday*. Click a document to open it.

<img class="shot shot-light" src="../screenshots/document-list-light.webp" width="1280" alt="The workspace documents shown as a list, with name, date and kind" />
<img class="shot shot-dark" src="../screenshots/document-list-dark.webp" width="1280" alt="The workspace documents shown as a list, with name, date and kind" />

- **Grid or list**: use the two buttons next to the workspace name. In the list, click **Name**, **Date Modified** or **Kind** to sort. Click again to reverse.
- **Documents from anywhere**: **File › Open…** (<kbd>⌘O</kbd>) opens a document from any folder, without moving it. You can also double-click it in Finder. A document from outside the workspace always opens in [a window of its own](#documents-outside-the-workspace).
- **Open Recent**: the **File** menu remembers what you opened last.

## Tabs

Each open document gets a tab, just like in a web browser.

<img class="shot shot-light" src="../screenshots/tabs-light.webp" width="1024" alt="Three documents open in tabs" />
<img class="shot shot-dark" src="../screenshots/tabs-dark.webp" width="1024" alt="Three documents open in tabs" />

- **Switch**: click a tab, or press <kbd>⇧⌘]</kbd> and <kbd>⇧⌘[</kbd>.
- **Close**: click the **×** or press <kbd>⌘W</kbd>. Closing the last tab takes you back to the list.
- **Back to the list**: your tabs stay above the list, so nothing closes. Click one to go back to that document.
- **Reorder**: drag a tab left or right.
- **Rename**: double-click the name in the tab, type, then press <kbd>↩</kbd>.
- **More options**: right-click a tab for **Move to New Window**, **Rename…**, **Copy Link**, **Close Tab**, **Close Other Tabs** and **Close All Tabs**. **Close All Tabs** closes every tab and takes you back to the list.

Each tab remembers where you were and what you can undo. Your tabs are still there the next time you open MarkPlus.

## Documents outside the workspace

A document from another folder isn't part of the workspace, so it always opens in a new window of its own:

- The window shows only that document, in a single tab. There's no **View workspace** button and no other tabs.
- <kbd>⌘W</kbd> closes the window.
- **Convert to .mdoc** keeps the document in its own folder: it doesn't move into the workspace.
- **New Document…** (<kbd>⌘N</kbd>) and links to workspace documents open in the workspace window.

If the document is already open, MarkPlus brings its window to the front.

## Two documents side by side

Drag a tab out of the window and drop it anywhere: it opens in its own window. You can also right-click a tab and choose **Move to New Window**.

**Example:** keep the research notes on the left and the report you're writing on the right.

A document can only be open in one window at a time. If you try to open it again, MarkPlus takes you to the window where it already is.

## Document icon

Give a document an icon to spot it at a glance. Point at the top of the document and click **Add icon**, then pick an emoji or an icon from a library, just as in a [text box](../text-boxes/#color-and-icon). The icon shows above the first line.

To change it, click the icon. **Remove** puts back the file icon.

The icon replaces the file icon everywhere the document appears: the document list, the pinned documents, the tabs, links to the document, and the [exported web page](../export/).

:::note
A `.mdoc` keeps its icon inside the file, so it stays when you rename or move the document. A `.md` file can't hold an icon, so MarkPlus remembers it in the workspace instead. Renaming it in MarkPlus keeps the icon; renaming or moving it in Finder loses it.
:::

Databases always show the database icon.

## Rename and delete

- The **file name is the title**. Rename it in MarkPlus or in Finder, whichever you prefer.
- Right-click a document in the list for **Rename…**, **Open in New Window** and **Delete**.
- **Delete** moves the document to the Trash, so you can still get it back.

## Saving

You don't need to save. MarkPlus saves a couple of seconds after you stop typing, and again when you switch documents or quit. If you like, <kbd>⌘S</kbd> saves right away.

## The bar at the bottom

- **Left**: where the document is on your Mac. Click the last folder to open it in Finder.
- **Right**: how many characters, words and paragraphs it has, and whether an AI app is connected.

Turn each side on or off in **View › Path Bar** and **View › Status Bar**.
